Sudoku Duel

Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This page explains how to delete your account and data in Sudoku Duel: Daily Puzzle (Turkish title: Sudoku Düello: Günlük Bulmaca), developed by MLK Craft.
Android package name: com.sahin.sudoku

01 Delete inside the game

If the game is installed on your phone, you can delete your account yourself in a few seconds:

  • Open the game and tap the ⚙️ Settings icon in the top-right corner.
  • In the Support & Account section, tap 🗑️ Delete Account.
  • In the window listing the data to be deleted, tap Yes, Delete My Account. If you signed in with Google, you may be asked to confirm your Google account once more for security.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

02 Delete by email if the game is not installed

If you can't access the game, send an email to info@mlkcraft.com with the subject "Sudoku Duel – Account Deletion Request" and include:

  • Your in-game player name (username),
  • If you signed in with Google, the linked Google email address.

Your account and data will be deleted within 30 days and we will confirm by email. We may ask for additional information to verify that the account belongs to you.

03 Data that is deleted

When your account is deleted, all of the following is permanently removed from our servers (Google Firebase) and your device:

  • Your game account (guest account or the account linked with Google),
  • Your player name and reserved username,
  • Your cloud save: coins, hints and bombs, statistics, daily quest and event progress, settings,
  • Your entries in the all-time and weekly leaderboards,
  • Your duel statistics and any matchmaking entry.

04 Data that is kept, and for how long

  • Duel records: Records of finished duels are deleted automatically when players leave the result screen; we keep no other copy.
  • Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase): Anonymous usage data that is not linked to your identity is kept by Google for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically.
  • Advertising data (Google AdMob): Data tied to your advertising ID is controlled by Google, not by us, and is kept under the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Purchase records: In-app purchase payments are processed by Google Play; order records on Google Play are not deleted because of legal accounting and tax obligations.

If you open the game again after deletion, a brand-new guest account is created. Deleted coins and progress cannot be restored.

For details on what data we collect and why, see our Privacy Policy.
